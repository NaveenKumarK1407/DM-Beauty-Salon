# DM Beauty Salon: initial SEO audit

Audit date: 5 September 2026. Target: https://dmbeautysalon.in, Medak, Telangana.

## Executive finding and evidence limits

The public HTTPS homepage returned HTTP 200 but served a Hostinger parked-domain page. Its title was `Parked Domain name on Hostinger DNS system`; its robots meta directive was `noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet`. This was verified by fetching public HTML, after the initial sandbox connection failed. Connecting the domain to the actual salon application is the first priority. This observation is from one network vantage point; DNS propagation can vary.

The local Next.js project is a separate audit surface: its existence does not prove deployment. No Google Search Console, Analytics, Google Business Profile, keyword-volume service, backlink dataset or localized Google Maps grid was available. Rankings, volumes, difficulty, review velocity, traffic, conversions, domain age and Core Web Vitals remain unmeasured. Search-tool results are discovery evidence, not Google rank measurements.

## 30 / 60 / 90 day operating plan

Days 1–7: connect domain, correct origin, verify business facts, remove unsupported claims, inspect live HTML/robots/sitemap/canonicals/404 behavior. Test phone, WhatsApp and booking on mobile. Launch is successful when customers reach accurate information and can contact the salon.

Days 8–30: complete GBP and Search Console baseline; collect permissioned photos and real service details. Review content on services and contact pages. Request an honest review consistently from customers regardless of satisfaction; no incentives, review gating or suggested keywords. Suggested message: “Thank you for visiting DM Beauty Salon. If you would like to share your experience, you can leave an honest review here: [verified review link].” Reply helpfully without exposing appointment details.

Days 31–60: publish one substantive bridal page if the owner supplies actual work, inclusions, duration, trial/travel terms and booking policies. Link it from homepage and services. Add another service page only if it serves a distinct customer need. Verify existing useful directory listings and correct inconsistent business details before creating more.

Days 61–90: improve pages with relevant impressions but weak CTR or positions 4–20. Seek genuine referrals/mentions from local photographers, wedding planners and nearby businesses. Outreach is a future owner task; no messages have been sent. Compare enquiries and bookings with baseline and focus effort on services that convert.

## Google guidance and corrections to existing SEO notes

Google explains local visibility through relevance, distance and prominence; no ranking guarantee follows from schema or service-area lists: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 . LocalBusiness markup should accurately describe the real business: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business . Google's guidance describes review markup for sites reviewing other businesses; do not restore self-serving rating markup expecting salon review stars.

The existing `SEO-MEDAK.md` is not reliable proof of live configuration. It contains unverified operational claims and historical statements, including null pricing, whereas current static data contains numeric prices. Reconcile documentation with current application behavior and owner-confirmed facts before launch.
